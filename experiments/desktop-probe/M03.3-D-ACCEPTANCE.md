## Accepted standalone behavior

An independent `desktop-probe --bring-here` caller captures the current desktop **before owner discovery** (two strict reads), freezes its exact target in the existing v2 caller marker, and validates owner/desktop once again before its only request send. Fixed caller budget **5000 ms**; owner operation budget **3500 ms**. An admitted Here uses the existing single `ActiveOperation`, strict destination workarea/viewport/retained-monitor proof, and at most one checked root-directed `_NET_WM_DESKTOP` request. The WM acknowledgement is never treated as proof of relocation.

A safe already-local body is a no-op. Visible remote confirmation retains the same body XID and effective Normal/Above/Below layer, verifies actual fixed-size geometry and input silhouette, and may apply at most one position-only correction with 150 ms confirmation. Genuine minimized Iconic+Unmapped+HIDDEN is preserved even when moved to the caller's desktop; success is qualified with **placement/input deferred** and never implicitly Shows the body. No focus/activation, user-workspace switch, automatic body following, monitor migration, layer change, rollback or second desktop send. Here Partial requires independently confirmed relocation history, not dispatch acknowledgement or local correction.

Existing Hide/Show/Bring Top remain independent and accepted. **`--recover` remains unknown in CLI and Unsupported in private owner/service admission.** Stage E composition is separate and not authorized by Stage-D acceptance.
